## Sequence and annotation records for the restricted locus audit

The restricted vertebrate mitochondrial tRNA-Phe(GAA) locus audit used accession-versioned records retrieved from the NCBI Nucleotide database. Accession identifiers and per-record validation summaries are provided in the public locus validation table; source nucleotide records remain hosted by NCBI. Feature-location interpretation follows the INSDC/DDBJ and ENA qualifier specifications:

- DDBJ feature table definition: https://www.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/ddbj/feature-table-e.html
- ENA anticodon qualifier reference: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/WebFeat/qualifiers/anticodon.html

These references support methods and claims described in the public resources. They do not establish that the descriptive comparison is a new method or a novel evolutionary result.
