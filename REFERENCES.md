This list identifies the principal literature, databases, software, and structural records used by the project. The manuscript bibliography will provide the complete citation set and will cite the specific claims made in the text.

- Knies JL, Dang KK, Joyce PBM, Assmann SM, Benfey PN, Wagner A. (2008). Substitution rate variation in the 5S ribosomal RNA gene family in Arabidopsis thaliana. *Molecular Biology and Evolution*. https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msn130
- Rivas E. (2021). Evolutionary conservation of RNA sequence and structure. *WIREs RNA*. https://doi.org/10.1002/wrna.1649
- Rivas E, Clements J, Eddy SR. (2017). A statistical test for conserved RNA structure shows lack of evidence for structure in lncRNAs. *Nature Methods*. https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4066
